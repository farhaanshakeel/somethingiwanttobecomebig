Welcome

Pi-space

A structured learning and studying community for students who want to get better at their Mathematics and Physics skills and stand out, while also preparing for examinations like olympiads, for example IOQM, RMO, or IMO, and more. It is a non-profit community, providing everything free of cost.

Join the discord community, the main place for community interaction.

Highlights

Lessons Lessons Lessons posted by the mentors. Click here to visit, learn, and provide feedback to the teacher. Programs Programs Programs launched by the community. Programs are the main feature of the community, with many benefits and sessions ongoing. Each program lasts a year, and there are sub-programs within it. Activities Activities Research-based activities, guided practice, and deadline-driven challenges that help students grow together.

How It Works

  1. Join the site with Discord for a secure student profile.
  2. Browse the academy tracks and open course pages.
  3. Use the bot for study groups, teacher posts, and guided practice.

What’s in the Academy

Lessons
