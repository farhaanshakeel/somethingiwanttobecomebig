How to Add a Course

  1. Post a lesson through the bot or publish the course outline through the academy backend.
  2. Include goals, prerequisites, class notes, and practice prompts.
  3. Add study group links or discussion prompts for students.

Schedule & Events

Community study sessions and lessons are scheduled by server moderators. If you run a lesson series, add events to your server calendar and share the invite link in the #events channel.
