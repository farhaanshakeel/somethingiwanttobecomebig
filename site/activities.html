Programs

Acharnement

Acharnement is a year-long program whose sole purpose is to enhance our abilities in both Mathematics and Physics by learning and growing together with our peers through a variety of sub-programs.

The program will officially begin once we reach our target of 100 members.

Points & Rewards

There will be a points system that can be converted into Power IQ credits, a community with an economy system where credits can be used to purchase various rewards.

Conversion rate: 5 points = 1 Power IQ credit

  • Solving the difficult weekly problems provided by the mentors.
  • Demonstrating legitimate improvement in your skills across different subjects.
  • Completing subject-related activities and challenges.
  • Performing well in examinations.
  • Teaching others or helping solve their doubts.

More details will be announced later in the server. Feel free to ask questions in the meantime.

What the Program Includes

Mentors

Each subject will have dedicated mentors. They will conduct lessons, quizzes, and engaging activities.

Activities

Research-based activities where you explore a topic in depth and present ideas, theories, methods, or postulations.

Study Groups

Members are free to create their own study groups, provided they ask the moderators first.

Exams & Moderated Exams

Main examinations are held every two months, alongside moderated exams for study groups.
