Terms, privacy & security

The rules, privacy practices, community standards, and security commitments for the Pi-space website and Discord bot.

Last updated: 2026-09-15

This page is written for the current Pi-space deployment. It is general service information, not legal advice. The person or organization operating a particular Pi-space deployment remains responsible for configuring it lawfully and keeping its contact information current.

Terms of Service

By using Pi-space, you agree to use the website, Discord bot, and related features lawfully and respectfully. Pi-space may be changed, paused, or discontinued. Features are provided on an availability basis and may contain errors.

Acceptable use

  • Do not use Pi-space to harass, threaten, defraud, impersonate, or target another person.
  • Do not upload malware, attempt unauthorized access, bypass authentication, probe private endpoints, or interfere with service availability.
  • Do not submit personal information about another person without a lawful reason and appropriate consent.
  • Server owners and moderators may remove content or access that violates these terms or their server rules.

Content and responsibility

You are responsible for content you submit through bot commands, profiles, lessons, or the editor. Do not publish confidential information, passwords, access tokens, or sensitive personal data. Educational content is provided for general learning and is not a substitute for professional advice.

Availability and liability

To the extent permitted by law, Pi-space is provided without warranties and its maintainers are not responsible for indirect losses, lost data, or interruptions caused by the service, Discord, hosting providers, or user configuration. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be limited.

Privacy Policy

Pi-space processes only the information needed to provide the enabled features. Depending on the deployment, this may include a Discord user ID, username, avatar information, server or channel identifiers, profile selections, lesson data, planner entries, study-group data, and website session information.

How information is used

  • To authenticate users through Discord and maintain a server-side login session.
  • To provide profiles, study groups, lessons, planning, and productivity features requested by users or server administrators.
  • To protect the service, investigate abuse, and maintain reliability.

Storage and sharing

The default project stores bot data in the deployment's local data store. The website stores temporary sessions in server memory. Discord and the hosting provider process information under their own policies. Pi-space does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when required by law or to protect users, the service, or the public.

Retention and choices

Data is retained only while needed for the enabled feature or deployment. You may request correction or deletion of data held by a specific server owner or deployment operator. Because deployments can be independently hosted, the request must be sent to the operator identified in the Contact section or to the relevant Discord server administrators.

Cookies

The website uses a secure, HTTP-only session cookie for login. It is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking. Clearing cookies signs you out of the current browser.

Security

Pi-space uses Discord OAuth instead of collecting Discord passwords. OAuth state is validated before login, sessions use unpredictable server-generated identifiers, editor mutations require the configured editor account and a session-bound CSRF token, and editor file writes are limited to the public site directory.

Your responsibilities

  • Keep Discord, hosting, repository, and deployment secrets private.
  • Never paste bot tokens, OAuth client secrets, or session cookies into profiles, lessons, issues, or chat.
  • Use HTTPS in production and set the documented CORS, cookie, and OAuth redirect settings.
  • Limit access to the configured site-editor Discord account and review published content.

Report a vulnerability

Do not publicly post exploitable details or credentials. Contact the deployment operator through the Contact section with the affected URL, reproduction steps, impact, and a safe way to follow up. Remove secrets from logs and rotate any credential that may have been exposed.

Community Guidelines

Pi-space is intended to support respectful learning. Harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing, non-consensual sharing of personal data, spam, malicious links, and disruptive behavior are not allowed. Moderators may act to protect the community and may apply additional server rules.

Contact

For policy questions, data requests, or security reports, contact the Pi-space owner or the administrators of the Discord server where you use the bot. Replace this paragraph with a monitored support email or official support channel before public launch.
